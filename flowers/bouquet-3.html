A handful of sunshine.

Yellow and peach flowers, surrounded by little clouds of white.

A little meaning in every bloom

Yellow carnations

In the language of flowers, the carnation speaks of fascination, distinction, and love, and the yellow shade sharpens that message.

When I give you these yellow carnations, I’m celebrating the way even an ordinary afternoon feels special with you.

Peach carnations

Peach carnations symbolize sincerity, modest gratitude, warmth, and gentle admiration.

When I give you these peach carnations, I’m thanking you for choosing to spend your time with me and for the kindness you bring into my life.

Baby’s Breath

Baby’s breath symbolises purity, everlasting love, innocence, sincerity, and new beginnings.

These little flowers are my way of saying I hope the small moments we share become memories we carry for a long time.
